What we do

Five things we set up for your shop.

Most new customers search on Google, look at your photos, hours and reviews, then tap to call or book. These five pieces work together so that customer ends up in your chair.

01

Every call answered

Busy, closed or at lunch? A phone assistant answers in your shop's name, books the appointment and texts you the details.

  • Answers after hours, weekends and in the rush
  • Books the appointment and texts you the details
  • Tells callers it's your virtual assistant, always

The fix: callers get an answer and a booking, even while your hands are busy.

02

More 5-star reviews

Every customer gets the same easy, one-tap review request after their visit, and every review gets a real, personal reply. Never just the happy ones: that's Google's rule and ours.

The fix: reviews that stopped piling up start again, automatically, after every visit.

03

Found first on Google

Your Google profile set up properly and kept fresh with posts, photos and answers, so the people searching nearby see you before the shop down the road.

The fix: the right hours, address and photos on your Google profile, so nearby searchers pick you, not the shop next door.

04

A website that books

Fast on every phone, with your real photos, prices and hours, and one tap to call, text or book. In English and Spanish when your customers need it.

The fix: a page of your own, so bookings don't depend on someone else's app or someone else's terms.

05

Results you can see, live

Your own page showing calls answered, bookings and new reviews, any time. No monthly PDF full of numbers that don't matter.

The fix: your numbers on one live page, so you catch a slide before it becomes a real problem.

FAQ

Questions shop owners ask.

Have another question? Text us. You'll get a clear, honest answer.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $149 a month (Starter) plus a one-time $297 setup. Growth is $297 a month plus $697 setup, and Pro is $497 a month plus $997 setup, with your ad budget paid to the ad platform. Websites on their own are $249, $449 or $649, paid once. Everything is month-to-month.

Will my customers know it's not a person?

Yes, and that's on purpose. Your assistant always says it's your shop's virtual assistant and that the call may be recorded. Callers care about getting an answer and a booking, and they get both.

What is the free homepage preview?

We build a preview of your homepage from your public Google listing and send it to your phone. It's free, nothing goes public, and you don't have to buy anything. See how the preview works.

Can I try the phone assistant myself?

Yes. Call our live demo at (727) 606-3663 and pretend you're one of your own customers. Ask about hours, prices or booking. It's the same kind of assistant we set up for shops, and it tells you up front that it's a virtual assistant.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. It's month-to-month, and you can cancel any time.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. It goes live on your existing number, and your number stays in your name.

Who owns everything?

You do. Your phone number, Google profile, ad account, website and customer list stay in your name from day one.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I need this?

Keep your Facebook page. This makes sure the people who find you on Google, or call you, actually get answered and booked. It all links together.

Who will I actually be working with?

Aren. Grand Blvd Studio is small on purpose, so you talk to the same person every time, from the first preview to the monthly check-in.

How long does a page take?

Most one-page sites go live within a week once we have your photos, hours and prices. Three- and five-page sites usually take two to three weeks.

What if I already have a site?

We can usually set up the phone assistant and review requests on your current site and number without rebuilding anything. A new site only happens if you want one.
