How it works · starts free

We call your shop first. Then we show you what we heard.

The Missed-Call Test is free. You see exactly what a new customer sees when they call you, before you spend a cent.

  1. Test

    We call your shop at your busiest times and note what happens: answered, voicemail, or nothing. You get a one-page report with what missed calls may be costing you, using your own prices.

  2. Build

    Like what you see? We set up your text-back, phone assistant and review requests in your shop's name. You hear it and approve every word before it goes live.

  3. Launch

    It goes live on your existing number. We test every call, text and booking on a real phone, and your live results page switches on.

  4. Grow

    Month-to-month. We tune it as the calls come in, reply to reviews, keep your Google profile fresh, and check in with you every month.

FAQ

Clear answers.

Have another question? Text us. You'll get a clear, honest answer.

How much does it cost?

The Growth System starts at $149 a month plus a one-time $297 setup. Growth is $297 a month plus $697 setup, and Pro is $497 a month plus $997 setup, with your ad budget paid to the ad platform. Websites on their own are $249, $449 or $649, paid once. Everything is month-to-month.

Will my customers know it's not a person?

Yes, and that's on purpose. Your assistant always says it's your shop's virtual assistant and that the call may be recorded. Callers care about getting an answer and a booking, and they get both.

What is the free Missed-Call Test?

We call your shop at busy times, note what happens, and send you a one-page report: what a new customer experiences, and what missed calls may be costing you at your own prices. It's free, and you don't have to buy anything.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. It's month-to-month, and you can cancel any time.

Who owns everything?

You do. Your phone number, Google profile, ad account, website and customer list stay in your name from day one.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I need this?

Keep your Facebook page. This makes sure the people who find you on Google, or call you, actually get answered and booked. It all links together.

Who will I actually be working with?

Aren. Grand Blvd Studio is small on purpose, so you talk to the same person every time, from the first test to the monthly check-in.
