What you get

  • A homepage made for your shop. We build it from your public Google listing: your services, your hours, and a booking or call button.
  • Your photos, if you send some. Text us a few pictures of your work or your space and we’ll use them.
  • A clear “Preview” label. It’s a preview, not a live site. It isn’t public, and it doesn’t go live until you say yes.

What we need from you

Just your shop’s name. That’s it. Photos are optional: if you have a few good ones, text them along with the name.

How it works

  1. You text us your shop’s name.
  2. We make the preview from your public Google listing, plus any photos you sent.
  3. You get a private link. Open it on your phone, the way your customers will.
  4. You tell us one thing to change, and then you decide. If it’s a no, that’s fine.

If you do want it live, the website prices are on our pricing section. Nothing is added to them for the preview.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The preview costs nothing, and you owe us nothing for looking at it. There is no obligation.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. If you like the preview, we'll tell you what it takes to put it live. If you don't, say so and that's the end of it.

Will it be public?

No. Preview pages sit on private links that search engines are told to skip. A preview is not live, and not findable, until you say yes.

What happens next?

You look at it on your phone, tell us one thing you'd change, and decide. That's the whole process.
