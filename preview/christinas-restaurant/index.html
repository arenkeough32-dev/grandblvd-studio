Our Story

One of downtown's oldest tables

1991. Christina's opened its doors on Main Street — and never left. The downtown business association counts it among the oldest establishments still serving the community on Main.

Today. It's still the family-owned breakfast house regulars know by name: Greek specialties next to American diner classics, breakfast served all day, a patio out front, and homemade soup every day.

The Menu

Breakfast all day — and then some

Christina's menu is published on SinglePlatform. These are the verified categories — the full menu with prices lives at the link below.

Breakfast — served all day

Eggs any style with home fries or grits, a dozen-plus omelets (Greek with feta, western, veggie, Mexican), pancakes, waffles, French toast, biscuits & gravy, huevos rancheros, and breakfast burritos.

Greek specialties

Gyros, chicken souvlaki, Greek salads topped with feta, and the Greek burger — grilled peppers, onions and feta. The Greek side of the menu is what sets Christina's apart from an ordinary diner.

Sandwiches & clubs

Triple-decker clubs, Reubens, patty melts, grilled cheese, hot-pressed Cuban, Italian combo, Philly steak, chicken parmesan and meatball parm.

Homemade favorites

Daily rotating homemade soups, burgers on 100% beef, liver & onions, open-face roast beef, chopped sirloin, and a fish platter.

Sides & sweets

Homemade rice pudding, apple, lemon-meringue and banana-cream pie — plus a kids' menu for the little ones.
