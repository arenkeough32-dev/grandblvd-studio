Our Building

Baked goods in a 1914 schoolhouse

1914. Elfers was a young citrus town when its new consolidated school opened — an elegant brick building at the corner of Barker Drive and Grey Street. Local students filled its classrooms for half a century.

1965–1979. After the school moved on, the old building faced an uncertain future — at one point it was condemned and nearly demolished. Community groups campaigned to save it, and CARES (Community Aging & Retirement Services) made it their Elfers senior center.

Today. After a careful restoration, the historic schoolhouse has reopened as the Elfers Center — and inside it, That Little Bakery Cafe carries on the tradition of a gathering place for the neighborhood. Have a coffee and a pastry where Elfers kids once learned their ABCs.
