Trinity books on phones, often after hours.

Trinity is full of busy households, and a lot of people find a new salon or barber on their phone in the evening, after work and after the kids are down. If the only way to book is to call during open hours, they move on to whoever lets them pick a time right then.

What we check and fix for Trinity shops

  • A “Book now” button that works at 10 PM, through the booking app you already use
  • Prices or price ranges, so nobody has to call just to ask
  • Your services listed on Google, so you show up for the exact thing they searched
  • Your booking link right on your Google profile, so people can book without calling

What we do

  • Websites made for phones. Your services, prices, hours, photos and a one-tap call or book button. Fast on any phone.
  • Your Google profile, set up properly. The right categories, your services, real photos and replies to your reviews.

Questions

Do you work with shops in Trinity?
