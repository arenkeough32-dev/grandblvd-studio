What you’re paying for

The thinking happens before the design.

A template can make a page look nice. It can’t tell which of your services people drive across town for. That part takes reading, listening and testing, and it’s where most of our time goes.

  1. We start with your reviews.Before we design anything, we read your Google reviews and note what clients praise most. That goes on your first screen, so new customers see your best work, not a price list.
  2. Written for your shop.No template with your name dropped in. Every line is written for your shop, in your voice, and you approve every word before anything goes live.
  3. Built for the phone in their hand.Most people find you on a phone, often in a hurry. Call, text and book stay within thumb reach the whole way down, and we test every button before launch.
  4. Easy for Google to read.Your hours, address, services and prices are set up so Google reads them correctly, and your website and Google profile point to each other.
  5. English and Spanish.A Spanish version when your customers need one, so more of your neighbors can read about you and book in their own language.
  6. Yours, always.Your web address, website, Google profile, phone number and ad account stay in your name. If you ever leave, you keep all of it.

Questions

About prices and plans.

Have another question? Text us. You’ll get a clear, honest answer.

What does the setup fee pay for?

The build: reading your reviews, writing everything, the design, connecting your phone, Google profile and booking app, and testing every call, text and button before launch. You pay it once.

Can I start small and add more later?

Yes. Many shops start with a website or the social starter pack and add a monthly plan when they’re ready.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Every plan is month-to-month, and you can cancel any time.

What happens if I cancel?

Your website, Google profile, phone number and ad account stay in your name. The monthly services stop, so the phone assistant stops answering and your calls ring the way they did before.

Why are your prices lower than a big agency’s?

We’re a small, new studio with no office to pay for, and we’d rather earn our first shops’ trust than charge agency rates. The work is held to the same standard as the Classic Styles site.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. The phone assistant goes live on your existing number, and your number stays in your name.

Who will I be working with?

Aren. Grand Blvd Studio is small on purpose, so you deal with the same person from the first preview to the monthly check-in.
