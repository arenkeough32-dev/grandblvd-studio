Good questions

I colored my hair at home and it went wrong. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Book a free consultation first. We’ll look at your hair in person, do a strand test and give you a plan and a price. Big corrections are sometimes done over two visits to keep your hair healthy.

How long does a balayage appointment take?

Plan on three to four hours, including toner, a cut if you want one, and a blowout. Longer or thicker hair can take a bit more.

¿Puedo hacer mi cita en español?

¡Sí! Marisol y Luz hablan español. Llámanos o menciona “español” cuando reserves en línea y te asignamos con una de ellas. Yes, two of our stylists speak Spanish, so just ask when you book.
