Questions

Anything else, just call or text (727) 555-0171.

How should I come to my appointment?

With clean, makeup-free lashes and no contact lenses if you can. You'll be lying back with your eyes closed for most of it, so many clients nap.

How long do extensions last?

Your natural lashes shed every day, so most people come in for a fill every two to three weeks. With good aftercare a set stays full and neat between visits.

What if I need to reschedule?

Give us 24 hours' notice and there's no charge. Late cancellations and no-shows forfeit the $25 booking deposit.
