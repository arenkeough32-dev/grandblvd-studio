Good questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome all day. Booking online holds your chair so you skip the wait, which helps on Fridays and Saturdays.

How long does a fade take?

Plan on 30 to 40 minutes for a fade, and about 50 minutes if you add a beard trim.

What if I'm running late?

Give us a call. We hold your spot for 10 minutes, then we'll fit you in at the next open chair.
