Questions we hear a lot

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is open. Weekends fill up fast, so booking online is the surest way to get your spot.

How long does a gel manicure last?

Most clients get two to three weeks. Pool and beach days are fine; we seal the edges so it holds up to salt water.

Can I bring a photo of a design I like?

Please do. Show us when you check in and we'll match it as closely as we can and give you the price before we start.
