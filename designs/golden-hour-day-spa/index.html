Before your visit

What should I wear, and what do you provide?

Come as you are. We provide a robe, sandals, a locker and everything you need for your treatment. For massage, undress to your comfort level; you’re always covered except for the area being worked on.

I’m pregnant. Can I still book?

Yes, after your first trimester. Book the prenatal massage and let us know how far along you are. We’ll set up the table with side-lying support and skip any treatments that aren’t recommended.

How do gift cards work?

Gift cards can be used for any treatment or add-on, they never expire, and any leftover balance stays on the card. Lost the email? Call us and we’ll look it up by name.
