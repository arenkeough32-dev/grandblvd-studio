Preguntas

Questions we get at the counter.

Do you cater parties?

Yes. Taco trays start at 24 tacos with salsas, onions, cilantro and limes. Text us at least two days ahead with the date and headcount.

Is there seating inside?

A few tables inside and a couple of picnic tables out back under the umbrellas. Most people grab it to go.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Rajas con queso tacos, cheese enchiladas, elote and the bean and cheese burrito. Our beans are cooked without lard.
