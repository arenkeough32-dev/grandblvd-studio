## Recording checklist (room noise, −60 dB target)

Aren's room measured ~−47 dB against a −60 dB pro spec in an earlier check — that gap is the #1 reason an audition/recording gets skipped. Free fixes, in order:

1. Smallest room with the most soft stuff (a walk-in closet with clothes beats any "studio").
2. Phone on airplane mode; unplug/quiet the fridge if it's in the same room; record at the quietest hour of the day.
3. Mouth 6–10 inches from the mic, slightly off-axis; record 20 seconds of room tone first.
4. Clean with free Audacity: Noise Reduction (profile from the room tone), then normalize to −3 dB peak.

Do this before recording, not after.
