## 1. The 10-minute pre-call check-up SOP (before EVERY booked call)
1. Google the business name + town. Screenshot the knowledge panel (hours, phone, review count).
2. Open the top 4 other sources: Yelp, Bing Maps, Apple Maps, Facebook. Compare hours + phone + address line by line.
3. Find ONE disagreement (hours, phone, address, name spelling, missing Saturday). Two sources that disagree = the hook.
4. Check review velocity: last Google review date. If >30 days, that's the second hook.
5. Write the one-line opener: "I checked [source A] and [source B] — they say two different things about [X]. Which is true?"
6. If NO disagreement found: open with review velocity or "how do new customers find you today?" — never fake a gap.
Time: 10 minutes. Output: one card per lead (Claude keeps these in the lead board "last check" column).

## 2. SMS auto-replies for (727) 312-7758

If no reply in 48h: "Last note from me — the list I made stays yours either way. If the hours thing bites you later, text me. — Aren"

## 3. Booking form fields (4 only — every extra field loses calls)
Business name · Town · Phone · "What's making you reach out?" (optional).
After submit: instant text-back using template #1 (Claude wires the form to the text line).
