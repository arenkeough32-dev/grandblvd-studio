How we run it: six parts

A website is step one. We run the rest.

We set your shop up to be found, booked and remembered, then keep it working every month. You run the shop, and you approve every word.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We start with your reviews.

    • A free preview of your homepage, on your own phone, before you pay anything
    • A review check: what your clients praise most, and what the top shops near you get reviewed for
    • An honest pick: the one or two things that would help your shop most

    Free preview for every shop. The review check comes with every monthly plan.

  2. 02

    Be found

    When someone nearby looks you up, your profile looks open, busy and looked after.

    • Your Google profile cleaned up: right categories, every service, real photos, correct hours and holiday hours
    • A fast website with your real prices as text Google can read, not a photo of a flyer
    • 4 Google posts a month, fresh photos and updated hours and services

    Growth and Pro (one-page website included). Websites on their own from $249; posts and updates on the $49 care plan.

  3. 03

    Booked

    Every way to reach you works, even when your hands are full.

    • A phone assistant that answers in your shop’s name, day or night, in English and Spanish
    • Call, Text and Book buttons that stay on screen the whole way down
    • Online booking connected to the app you already use, and a “Book now” button on your Google profile

    Phone assistant: Growth and Pro. Online booking: the $649 five-page website.

  4. 04

    Reviews

    More five-star reviews, and every one answered.

    • A friendly review request after every visit, to every client, never only the happy ones
    • A personal reply to every review, in your voice
    • Tough reviews: we draft a calm reply and check it with you first

    Every plan, from Starter at $149 a month.

  5. 05

    Videos and ads

    Ads that look like the place people will walk into.

    • A shot list made for your shop: about 20 minutes of filming on your phone
    • 3 short videos to start, then 2 new ones every month, with captions and music cleared to use
    • Local ads in your own ad account, aimed at your slow days and checked every week

    Pro. Your ad budget is yours, paid to the ad platform. Want posts without ads? Try the $149 social starter pack.

  6. 06

    Your numbers

    Real numbers every month, in plain words.

    • Your live results page, open any time
    • What happened: new reviews, your rating next to 3 nearby shops, a summary of every call, and how many people tapped to call, book or get directions
    • A check-in every month, with the same person every time

    Every plan includes the results page and the monthly check-in. Call summaries come with Growth; ad results with Pro.
