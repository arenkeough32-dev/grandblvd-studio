Terms

Our terms, in plain words.

How working with us works: what happens first, what you pay for, and what stays yours.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

Grand Blvd Studio is a small studio in New Port Richey, Florida. Aren runs it, so you deal with the same person from the first preview to the monthly check-in. These terms cover our websites, monthly plans and the social starter pack.

It starts with a free preview

We build a preview of your homepage from your public Google listing and send it to your phone. It’s free, there’s no obligation, and nothing goes public. You only pay if you decide to go ahead.

Prices and payment

  • Our prices are listed on our Pricing page, in US dollars.
  • Websites and the social starter pack are paid once. Monthly plans have a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee.
  • A website on its own stays online and up to date with the $49 care plan, month to month. Your web address renews once a year, in your name.
  • If you buy a website and add Growth or Pro within 90 days, $249 comes off that plan’s setup fee.
  • On the Pro plan, the ad budget is yours. You pay it straight to the ad platform, not to us.
  • Anything marked “Ask for a quote” on the Pricing page is priced before we start.
  • Payment terms, including when you pay, are written on your quote before work starts. We send invoices by email, and you pay by Zelle.
  • Once work has started, payments aren’t refunded. The one exception is the social starter pack promise below.

Your OK comes first

  • You approve every word before anything goes live. Nothing is published without your OK.
  • Every website includes 2 rounds of changes. One round is one list of changes, sent together. If you need more than that, we’ll agree it with you first.
  • The build times on our Pricing page are the usual times. They start once we have your photos, hours and prices.

Monthly plans

  • Every plan is month-to-month, with no long contract. You can cancel any time by text or email. Your plan keeps running until the end of the month you’ve already paid for, and nothing more is charged after that.
  • If you cancel, the monthly services stop. If your plan has the phone assistant, it stops answering and your calls ring the way they did before.
  • What each plan includes, and what the website care plan includes, is listed on our Pricing page.
  • On the care plan, small changes like prices, hours, specials, photos and short text edits are included, up to about an hour of work a month. Bigger jobs, like a new page or a redesign, are quoted first and only start with your OK.

What’s yours

  • Your web address, Google profile, phone number and ad account stay in your name. If you ever leave, you keep all of it.
  • Your website is yours once it’s paid for.

Social starter pack

The pack is made only from your real photos. Our promise: if you use fewer than 6 of the 12 posts in your first month, tell us and we’ll refund you.

What we can’t promise

We do careful work to help your shop get found and booked. But Google, Facebook and other platforms decide what they show and to whom, and your customers decide whether to call. So we can’t guarantee a ranking, a number of reviews, calls, bookings or new customers.

Your information

How we handle your information, and your customers’ information, is on our Privacy page.

Florida law

Florida law applies to these terms.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
