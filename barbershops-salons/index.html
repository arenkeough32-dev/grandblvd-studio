Barbershops & salons

Websites and Google profiles for barbershops and salons in Pasco County

Most new customers find a barber or stylist the same way: a quick search on Google, a look at the photos and hours, then one tap to call or book. We build the website and the Google profile that make that easy, and the simple systems that keep reviews coming in. Everything is month to month, and you own every account.

The problem, from the street

Someone searches for a barber or a salon near them. Your listing shows old hours, a few dark photos and no Website button, so they tap the shop next to you instead. Nobody tells you it happened, and it can happen every day.

What we set up

A website that books

Fast, mobile-first sites with your real photos, prices and hours, from $248.88 for one page. You own the domain and the site.

Google profile care

Correct hours, a Website button that goes to your own site, a booking button, current photos and posts. It's the first thing most new customers see, often before your website.

Review requests and replies

A one-tap review request goes out after each visit, to every customer, never only the happy ones. Every review gets a personal reply. Shops with fresh, replied-to reviews look alive on Google; shops without them look closed.

A phone assistant that answers and books

On busy hours or after close, a virtual assistant answers in your shop's name, tells callers it's a virtual assistant, answers questions about hours and prices, and books or holds a time. You get a text summary of every call. Call our live demo at (727) 606-3663 and pretend you're one of your own customers.

How we work

  1. Preview. We build a free homepage preview from your public Google listing and send it to your phone.
  2. Build. If you want to go ahead, we finish the site and set up only the pieces you need.
  3. Launch. Everything runs in your name, on your accounts.
  4. Grow. A live results page shows calls answered, bookings and reviews earned, month by month.

Proof we can point to

Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa, the salon we work out of on Grand Blvd in New Port Richey, was our first site: a fast, bilingual site built to bring in more bookings for the salon's best work. You can see it at classicstylesdayspas.com and read the details on our homepage.

Where we work

New Port Richey, Port Richey, Hudson, Holiday, Trinity and the rest of Pasco County. Setup is done by phone, text and video, so it's quick and fits around your chair time.

Questions

Do I need a new website to use the phone assistant?

No. The phone assistant works with the phone number you already have. A website is a separate, optional piece.

Will my customers know they're talking to an assistant?

Yes, on purpose. The assistant always says it's your shop's virtual assistant. Callers care about getting an answer and a time, and they get both.

I already use a booking app. Does this replace it?

No. We point callers and texts to the booking app you already use, so your calendar stays in one place.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel any time. You keep your number, profile, site and customer list.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $148.88 a month (Starter) plus a one-time $296.88 setup. Websites alone are $248.88, $448.88 or $648.88, paid once. Full details are on our plans and prices page.
