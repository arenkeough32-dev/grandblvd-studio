Barbershops & salons

Websites and missed-call systems for barbershops and salons in Pasco County

Barbers and stylists lose customers in one predictable place: the phone rings while their hands are busy. We build the simple systems that catch those customers, and the website that turns a Google search into a booking. Everything is month to month, and you own every account.

The problem, from the chair

You're mid-fade or halfway through a color. The phone rings. You can't stop, so it goes to voicemail, and most new callers don't leave one. They call the next shop on Google. Multiply that by a busy Saturday and it adds up fast, and none of it shows up anywhere.

What we set up

Missed-call text-back

When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a friendly text within seconds: your shop's name, your hours, and a link to book or a promise that you'll call back. The customer stays yours instead of drifting to the next listing.

A phone assistant that answers and books

On busy hours or after close, a virtual assistant answers in your shop's name, tells callers it's a virtual assistant, answers questions about hours and prices, and books or holds a time. You get a text summary of every call. Call our live demo at (727) 606-3663 and pretend you're one of your own customers.

Review requests and replies

A one-tap review request goes out after each visit, to every customer, never only the happy ones. Every review gets a personal reply. Shops with fresh, replied-to reviews look alive on Google; shops without them look closed.

Google profile care

Correct hours, a Website button that goes to your own site, a booking button, current photos and posts. It's the first thing most new customers see, often before your website.

A website that books

Fast, mobile-first sites with your real photos, prices and hours, from $249 for one page. You own the domain and the site.

How we work

  1. Test. We run a free Missed-Call Test: three calls at your busy hours, notes only, plus a check of your Google profile.
  2. Build. If you want to go ahead, we set up only what the test showed you need.
  3. Launch. Everything runs in your name, on your accounts.
  4. Grow. A live results page shows calls caught, texts sent and reviews earned, month by month.

Classic Styles Salon & Day Spa, the salon we work out of on Grand Blvd in New Port Richey, was our first site: a fast, bilingual site built to bring in more bookings for the salon's best work. You can see it at classicstylesdayspas.com and read the details on our homepage.

Where we work

Questions

Do I need a new website to use missed-call text-back?

No. Text-back and the phone assistant work with the phone number you already have. A website is a separate, optional piece.

Will my customers know they're talking to an assistant?

Yes, on purpose. The assistant always says it's your shop's virtual assistant. Callers care about getting an answer and a time, and they get both.

I already use a booking app. Does this replace it?

No. We point callers and texts to the booking app you already use, so your calendar stays in one place.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel any time. You keep your number, profile, site and customer list.

What does it cost?

The Growth System starts at $149 a month plus a one-time $297 setup. Websites alone are $249, $449 or $649, paid once. Full prices are on our pricing section.
