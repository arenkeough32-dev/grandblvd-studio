Privacy and texting

Privacy, calls and texts.

Plain words about what we keep, why, and how to make us stop.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What we collect

If you text, call or email us, we keep your name, business name, phone number, email address and what you told us, so we can answer you and follow up.

Our demo phone line

Our live demo line at (727) 606-3663 is answered by a virtual assistant. It says so at the start of every call and tells you the call may be recorded. Calls are recorded and transcribed so we can follow up with you. If you'd rather we not keep your call, tell the assistant or text us, and we'll delete it.

Texts

We only text people who texted us first or asked us to. We don't send marketing texts without written consent. Reply STOP to any text from us and we'll stop right away.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services we use to run calls, texts, email, bookings and the preview form for you.

Systems we set up for shops

When we set up a phone assistant or review requests for a shop, the shop owns those accounts and its customers' information. We only use it to run the service the shop asked for.

What this website loads

No ads, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Our pages don't set cookies, and the fonts, pictures and videos come from our own site. Two exceptions:

  • Our sample design pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you open one.
  • Our Book a call page shows Google's appointment calendar, so your browser connects to Google there too. If you book a call, Google handles the booking under its own privacy policy, and we see the details you type into the booking form.

The free preview form

When you send the form on our Free preview page, your details are locked (encrypted) in your browser before they leave it. They pass through a notification service, ntfy.sh, and only we hold the key that opens them. A second short alert tells us a request came in. It carries none of your details.

Our Text us page only builds a text message on your phone. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your own text app.

Small notes kept in your browser

A few small notes stay in your own browser: the tag of the link or sign that brought you here (if you then text us from the site, that tag is added to your message so we know which link worked), whether you paused the moving parts of the page, and the times you sent the preview form, so it isn't sent twice by mistake. The first two clear when you close the tab. You can clear all of them in your browser settings.

Ask, change or delete

To see, correct or delete what we have about you, text (727) 312-7758, call (727) 606-3663, or email hello@grandblvdstudio.com. We'll answer within two business days.
