Privacy and texting

Privacy, calls and texts.

Plain words about what we keep, why, and how to make us stop. Last updated November 9, 2026.

What we collect

If you text, call or email us, we keep your name, business name, phone number, email address and what you told us, so we can answer you and follow up.

Our demo phone line

Our live demo line at (727) 606-3663 is answered by a virtual assistant. It says so at the start of every call and tells you the call may be recorded. Calls are recorded and transcribed so we can follow up with you. If you'd rather we not keep your call, tell the assistant or text us, and we'll delete it.

Texts

We only text people who texted us first or asked us to. We don't send marketing texts without written consent. Reply STOP to any text from us and we'll stop right away.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services we use to run calls, texts and email for you.

Systems we set up for shops

When we set up a phone assistant or review requests for a shop, the shop owns those accounts and its customers' information. We only use it to run the service the shop asked for.

Fonts

The fonts on this site are hosted on our own server. The one exception is our sample design pages: they load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when you open one.

Ask, change or delete

To see, correct or delete what we have about you, text (727) 312-7758, call (727) 606-3663, or email hello@grandblvdstudio.com. We'll answer within two business days.
